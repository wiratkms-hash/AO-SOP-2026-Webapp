Step 0B

Pre-Campaign Readiness & Kick-off Sync

AO AE MP

This step applies to Tier S, A, and B campaigns only. Tier C and Tier D campaigns skip this step entirely.

Technical Readiness Check

This applies to lower funnel campaigns: Conversion, Lead Gen, CPAS, and Application.

Deadline

AE/Planner must send a Tech Audit request in Slack at least 14 business days before the campaign starts.

Slack Thread Label for Tech Audit

"INQUIRY - Request to review and implementation for the conversion campaign tracking"
Forbidden

If the Tech Check fails, no campaign setup request can be made. The issue must be fully resolved first.

Meeting Rules

Campaign TypeKick-off Timing Requirement
New campaign with new SOsAt least 3 business days before setup request
Lower funnel campaignsAt least 14 business days before campaign start
AWO (Always-on)Short kick-off at start of each month — at least 3 BD before new campaigns
Required

Always invite planner-as-team-meeting@anymindgroup.com to every kick-off meeting.

After Kick-off — AO PIC Must Post in Slack

AO PIC must post a summary in the Slack MEDIA PLAN thread after the kick-off:

"AO Confirm ✅ Timeline confirmed..."
Forbidden

If there is no in-meeting summary AND no Slack message after the meeting, the kick-off is not considered complete.

Step 1

AE Request

AE — Primary MP — Provide Ad Sheet

AE initiates the setup request in Slack. The request must be complete and correctly formatted. Incomplete requests will not be processed.

Slack Thread Name

Open a new Slack thread using this format:

SETUP - (Country)_Campaign name_Platform_CAM ID_Duration

Example:

SETUP - (TH)_YumYumSD2025_Meta_CAM123456789_Nov-Dec2025

Request Must Include All of These

  • Hubspot Link
  • Setup row range (in Ad Sheet)
  • Number of campaigns and campaign objective
  • Start date and End date
  • Ad Sheet link — use the "Get link to this cell" function: Ad Request Sheet
  • Screenshot of the media plan
Forbidden

Without both SO Sign and Hubspot link, the AO team will not process any request. Both are required before any work can begin.

Cut-off Times — Tier S, A, B (Same-Day Execution)

Number of CampaignsCut-off Time
1–5 campaignsBefore 16:00
6–10 campaignsBefore 15:00
More than 10 campaignsNotify AO PIC at least 2 business days in advance
Note

If the request is submitted after the cut-off time, work will be done the next business day. No exceptions.

Tier C and D

Deadline

Tier C and D must submit the request 2 business days in advance. There is no same-day priority. No exceptions.

Step 2

AO Acknowledge

AO — Primary

AO PIC confirms receipt of the setup request and gives a clear delivery ETA. This keeps the AE and Media Planner informed.

Acknowledge Requirements

Deadline

AO PIC must reply within 2 hours in the same Slack thread, with a clear ETA for Ad Preview delivery.

Reply Template

"Received ✅ — @AE_Name, Ad preview will be sent by 16:00 today."

Rules

  • Reply in the original thread only. Keep all history in one place.
  • State the ETA clearly. Do not use vague language.
Forbidden

If the account brief does not specify enabling special features (such as Advantage+ on Meta), do NOT enable them under any circumstances.

Step 4

Optimization & Monitoring

AO — Primary MP — Communication point AE — CC'd only

AO PIC monitors performance daily and takes action when needed. All communication goes to the Media Planner directly.

Daily Tasks — AO PIC

  • Check Performance ad alert emails twice a day — morning and evening
  • Update the Tracking Sheet (Spend pacing / Achievement rate / Date pacing) before noon every day
  • Log every optimization action in the Optimization Action Log

Slack Performance Update Frequency (REPORT thread)

TierUpdate Frequency
Tier SEvery day
Tier AEvery day
Tier B3 times per week
Tier C2 times per week
Tier D1 time per week
Required

Even if the update frequency is lower, AO PIC must monitor performance every day. No exceptions.

Sync Sessions

TierSession Type
Tier SWeekly update session
Tier ABi-weekly update session (on days agreed at kick-off)

Communication Rule

Required

All optimization and technical discussions go directly to the Media Planner. AE is only CC'd. AE must not be the primary contact for optimization topics.

Key Principle

Important

Negotiating a margin reduction is NOT optimization. It is the last option. Use it only after all other optimization methods have been tried.

Appendix A

Kick-off Checklist

AO AE MP

All 11 agenda items must be covered in every kick-off meeting. Do not end the meeting until all items are confirmed.

Open Kick-off Template

  • SO Sign check + Campaign ID confirmed
  • TikTok client info (if applicable): company name, industry, website
  • Objective and KPI confirmed. Achievement Rate (ceiling ACM%, overall and per-campaign) agreed.
  • Campaign Structure (CBO/ABO, budget split, objective, targeting, tactic) — Mandatory
  • Way of Optimization Alignment — what AO can adjust: audience, age, budget, placement, etc.
  • Report format and cadence confirmed. (Looker for Tier S/A only; final vs. in-flight)
  • Optimization and Sync cadence — specify exact days and times. (Tier S/A only)
  • Timeline — flag conflicts with other clients and resolve in this session
  • Communication mapping — which Slack thread is used for what
  • Risk and Mitigation — creative delays, other timeline risks
  • Material and Access readiness — KOL page access, gencodes, client page, paid partnership, creative, UTM

Double-Check Guideline

AO AE MP

  • Budget optimization type (CBO vs. ABO)

  • Page identity
